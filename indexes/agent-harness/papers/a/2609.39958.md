## 摘要摘录

Corporate and investment banking teams use presentations to support credit decisions and advise clients on financing and transactions. Producing these decks requires reconciling financial data, tracing sources and turning analysis into a recommendation. We retrospectively study the development of an agentic harness combining a 27B language model, financial calculations, narrative templates and validation checks.
