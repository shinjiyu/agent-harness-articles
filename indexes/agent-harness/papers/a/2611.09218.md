## 摘要摘录

Automated harness optimization improves agents by learning from trajectories and task feedback. Existing offline methods use reference answers to optimize harnesses for reuse on held-out tasks. However, many real-world tasks are open-ended, while long runs expose new difficulties that offline-optimized harnesses may not address. To study harness optimization in these settings, we introduce PerfRace, a benchmark for long-running performance optimization of software projects, and investigate whether agents can use task-specific experience to recursively improve their harnesses during an ongoing task.
